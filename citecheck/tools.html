CiteCheck and other tools

CiteCheck AU is a free triage step, not a replacement for a citator or a research platform.

Other tools

ToolWhat it is

What CiteCheck does differently

  • It checks every citation in a whole document at once, and says for each one why it reached its result.
  • It runs in your browser on an open snapshot: the document is never uploaded, and offline mode sends nothing at all.
  • It compares party names with the citation, which catches a real citation attached to the wrong case.
  • It says plainly when it cannot check something, instead of treating silence as a pass.
  • Its accuracy will be published with the size of the test set and error margins (evaluation in progress).

What it cannot do

  • Tell you whether a case says what it is cited for, or whether a quotation is accurate.
  • Tell you whether a case is still good law, or a provision still in force for the relevant date.
  • Check Victorian, Queensland, WA, SA, Tasmanian, ACT or NT case law, or anything decided after 10 March 2025.
  • Check the section numbers of state Acts (titles only), or legislation of Victoria, the ACT or the NT.
  • Read citations in forms it does not recognise. A citation it does not list was not checked.

Every result is a lead for a person to follow up in the official source.
