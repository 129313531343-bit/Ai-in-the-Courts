AI in Australian Courts

Open data on generative AI in Australian litigation: a tracker of decisions where AI-generated false or inaccurate citations reached a court or tribunal, a matrix of court rules on AI, and a one-page checklist for checking citations before filing.

Release 1, built from 112 Australian decisions in Charlotin's database. Each row says how it was checked; rows not yet checked show only facts read from the judgment and Charlotin's own coding.
